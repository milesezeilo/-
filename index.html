About

Business development for sustainability businesses

I work with sustainability consultancies and clean energy businesses on business development: messaging, lead research, and tested email and call sequences that turn the right people into conversations.

I understand what I am selling. I worked on ESG data governance and training at Deloitte, supported sustainability clients in London through Subak, wrote ESG case studies focused on Africa, and managed solar projects for my family's business in Lagos. Deloitte's in-house training also gave me a grounding in sales frameworks like BANT.

Most recently, I have been business lead at Green Bison Digital, running lead generation and building AI tools for solar and energy installers, and a contract business development lead for The Sustainability Practice, where I designed and tested a business development programme, including the messaging behind it.

I hold a BA from Howard University (Magna Cum Laude) and have completed my MBA in Marketing Management at Greenwich Business School.
